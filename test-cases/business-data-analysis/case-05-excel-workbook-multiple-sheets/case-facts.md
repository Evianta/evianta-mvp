# Case 05 — Excel workbook with multiple sheets

**Case ID:** SYNTH-DATA-005  
**Input:** `quarterly-sales.xlsx`

A finance team shares a quarterly review workbook with three sheets:

- `Summary`: a few text lines about who prepared the report (no sales data).
- `Sales Data`: 40 rows, 8 quarters (2024-Q1 to 2025-Q4) × 5 categories with `quarter`, `category` and `revenue`.
- `Targets`: a short table of quarterly targets (a decoy that is not sales data).

**Suggested prompt:** Analyze my sales spreadsheet, highlight trends and anomalies, and create a dashboard.

This case checks that the right sheet is analyzed and that evidence names the sheet.
