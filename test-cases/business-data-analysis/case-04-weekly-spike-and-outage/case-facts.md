# Case 04 — Weekly spike and outage

**Case ID:** SYNTH-DATA-004  
**Input:** `sales-data.csv` (60 rows: 20 ISO weeks × 3 channels)

An e-commerce manager exports weekly orders and revenue by channel for weeks 1 to 20 of 2026. Something unusual happened in two weeks and she wants the analysis to find them without being told which ones.

**Suggested prompt:** Analyze my sales spreadsheet, highlight trends and anomalies, and create a dashboard.
