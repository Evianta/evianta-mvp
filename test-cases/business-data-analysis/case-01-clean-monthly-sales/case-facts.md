# Case 01 — Clean monthly sales

**Case ID:** SYNTH-DATA-001  
**Input:** `sales-data.csv` (48 rows: 12 months × 4 products)

Evergreen Office Supplies (fictional) sells four products. The finance lead exports FY 2025-26 monthly sales (April 2025 to March 2026) and wants a quick read on how the year went, which product drives revenue, and a dashboard to share.

**Suggested prompt:** Analyze my sales spreadsheet, highlight trends and anomalies, and create a dashboard.

This is the clean baseline case: no missing values, duplicates or format problems.
