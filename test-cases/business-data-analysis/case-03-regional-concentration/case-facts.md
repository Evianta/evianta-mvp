# Case 03 — Regional concentration

**Case ID:** SYNTH-DATA-003  
**Input:** `sales-data.csv` (96 rows: 12 months × 4 regions × 2 products)

A regional sales head exports FY 2025-26 sales of ergonomic chairs and standing desks across four regions. Total revenue looks flat, and she suspects the mix is shifting.

**Suggested prompt:** Analyze my sales spreadsheet, highlight trends and anomalies, and create a dashboard. Which regions are we depending on?
